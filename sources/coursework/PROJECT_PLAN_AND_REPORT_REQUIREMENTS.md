# ECSP3000 Project Plan and Report Requirements

This file preserves the key requirements supplied from the ECS Moodle pages. Check Moodle for the authoritative current wording.

## Project Plan

### Activities and Methodology
- Maximum one side of A4, approximately 300 words.
- Brief summary of practical and analytical methods proposed or investigated.
- Explain suitable tools, techniques and approaches and why they are suitable.
- Include equipment/software requirements and relevant ethical, health and safety, or environmental aspects.

### Plan and Milestones
- Initial plan of work in the form of a PERT chart with an integrated milestone list.
- Emphasise relationships/dependencies between activities and objective endpoints.
- Use a non-linear plan to show optional/stretch aspects and flexibility.

## Progress Report
- Account of technical progress made by the end of Semester 1.
- Body typically below 3000 words (roughly 10 A4 pages of text, excluding figures/front matter as appropriate).
- Possible content includes enhanced project description, background literature, proposed methodology, work completed, preliminary experiments and evaluation methodology.

## Intended audience
Write for another student who has just completed Year 2 of the same degree and could continue the project.

## General formatting
- A4 portrait, double-sided, single-spaced.
- 12 pt proportionally spaced serif font (e.g. Palatino, Times, Computer Modern).
- Binding margin >= 35 mm.
- Top, bottom and non-binding margins >= 24 mm.
- Paragraphs non-indented and separated by an empty line.
- Formal, non-conversational style.
- Number chapters/sections in legal style (e.g. 2.3.1); appendices lettered.

## Typical progress/final report structure
1. Title page
2. Abstract (<=200 words)
3. Statement of Originality
4. Acknowledgements (optional)
5. Table of Contents
6. Main chapters
7. References
8. Appendices

## Assessment emphasis
The supplied assessment criteria explicitly assess project management/professionalism, presentation and background/specification in Semester 1. The final report additionally places substantial emphasis on methods/techniques, analysis/evaluation, knowledge/understanding and achievement.
